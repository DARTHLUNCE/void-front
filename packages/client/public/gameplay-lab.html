How to read this evidence

A timeout is not a successful siege. Base damage and exposure timings separate reaching a base from finishing it. Class ship/base damage, healing and capture work are shown together (node damage is not recorded in this table): kill counts alone do not measure support value.

Positions are sampled every 30 simulated seconds; playback is a tactical trace, not an animation of live ship movement. Tick timing excludes diagnostic sampling and is sensitive to concurrent CPU work. Human feel and multiplayer balance still need playtesting.
