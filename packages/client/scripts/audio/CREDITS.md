# Audio material credits

Original sound design uses transformed CC0 field recordings. No film, game or music recordings were sampled. All 44 sources are itemized below; sources.json records exact download URLs, SHA-256 checksums, sizes and license URLs.

The dry-ice and hospital-bed servo sources are public HQ MP3 previews; blast-auto and blast-magnum are official MP3 downloads. These four inputs are lossy: decoding to WAV does not restore lost fidelity. The other 40 source files are original WAV downloads. The measured reflection response is credited separately below.
